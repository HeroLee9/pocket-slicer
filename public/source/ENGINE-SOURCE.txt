Toolchain: Emscripten 3.1.74. The bridge exposes one-slicer-api 0.7.0.
OrcaWasm requires a threaded WebAssembly host with SharedArrayBuffer and
cross-origin isolation.

Published runtime files are the unmodified release slicer-mt.js and
slicer-mt.wasm. The WASM is stored as two pieces only to fit static hosting
per-file limits. Concatenating slicer-mt.wasm.part0 and slicer-mt.wasm.part1
recreates the original 29,019,444-byte binary. Exact artifact hashes and source
provenance are available at ../engine/PROVENANCE.json.
